Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Application purpose

UPII-FX32 Research Archive is a privately operated research archiving application using rclone and the Google Drive API.

2. Google user data

The application accesses Google Drive files and folders authorized by the account owner to transfer, organize, verify, and archive research and administrative data.

3. Data use and disclosure

Google user data is used only for the operator's authorized archive operations. The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Research archives are not made publicly accessible through this website.

4. Authentication and security

Authentication is performed through Google OAuth. OAuth credentials and tokens are maintained locally in the operator's protected rclone configuration. They are not published in this repository.

5. Storage and deletion

Archived files are stored in the account owner's Google Drive. The operator controls their retention and deletion. Revoking the application's access is possible through Google Account security settings.

6. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact the operator through the support address displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
