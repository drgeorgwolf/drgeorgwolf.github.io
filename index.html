UPII-FX32 – Private Research Archive

Private technical information page for a personal scientific research archive.

Purpose

This application uses rclone and the Google Drive API to transfer and archive research and administrative files between authorized local computers and the owner's personal Google Drive storage.

Access and data handling

Access is restricted to the authorized Google account of the archive operator.

Google Drive authorization is used to perform file operations requested by the operator. This website does not provide public access to archived research files.

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored locally and are not published on this website.
